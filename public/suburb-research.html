Read the metrics correctly

  • Median price: suburb-level House or Unit median held in the current market dataset; it is not an estimate for an individual property.
  • Gross yield and vacancy: suburb screening inputs. Confirm current rent and vacancy evidence before making a decision.
  • Future Opportunity: a relative 0–100 screening index for a 3–5 year horizon, not a price forecast or guaranteed return.
  • Component scores: relative model signals. Missing components remain unavailable and are never shown as zero.
Data provenance and model identity

Market fields come from the current suburb_metrics dataset. Future Opportunity uses the version and horizon returned by the live API. Dates shown on each card are the API dataUpdated value. Source schedules vary, so confirm time-sensitive evidence independently.
