投资阅读 / Investment reading

现有证据显示，Oakleigh是一个成熟住宅市场：2024年独立屋中位价为131.1万澳元，过去五年年化增长3.55%，但2024年同比下降1.90%。2025年许可口径下净新增住宅为16套。租金和失业率来自覆盖范围更大的统计区域，应作为区域背景阅读，而不是Oakleigh精确观测。

The verified evidence describes a mature housing market: the 2024 house median was $1.311 million, five-year CAGR was 3.55%, while the one-year change was -1.90%. The 2025 permit-based net addition was 16 dwellings. Rent and unemployment measures use broader statistical areas and should be read as context rather than exact Oakleigh observations.

This is an evidence summary, not a recommendation, price forecast or personal financial advice.
