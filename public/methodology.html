Transparent methodology

Evidence first. Limits stated clearly.

AusHomeValue combines property facts, comparable sales and suburb-level signals to support early research. It is a screening and research tool—not a formal valuation, financial advice or development approval.

Last reviewed: 16 July 2026

Use the result as a starting point. Confirm important decisions with current contracts, title and planning documents, an appropriately qualified valuer, conveyancer, planner, lender or financial adviser.

1. Indicative property estimate

The estimate is generated from the information available for the address, including property type and attributes, recent market evidence and comparable transactions. The displayed range reflects uncertainty in the available evidence. A narrow range does not guarantee a sale price.

Comparable sales

Potential comparables are considered using factors such as proximity, transaction recency, property type, land and building characteristics where available. Missing, stale or inconsistent attributes can reduce confidence. Renovation quality, condition, views, easements, tenancy and off-market terms may not be captured.

Confidence

Confidence is an evidence-quality indicator, not a probability that a property will sell within the range. It may improve when better property facts or relevant documents are available.

2. Future Opportunity Score

The 0–100 score is a relative screening signal used to compare suburbs. It is not a forecast of capital growth or a promise of return. The model considers available indicators across value, yield, demand, supply, amenity, schools, infrastructure and data confidence. Indicator availability and freshness vary by location.

What the score can do

Help shortlist locations, compare broad trade-offs and identify questions for deeper due diligence.

What it cannot do

Predict individual sale prices, interest rates, policy changes, construction costs or future investment performance.

3. Planning and development signals

Zoning, overlays and development-related signals are indicative summaries only. Planning controls change and site-specific constraints may not be visible. Always verify the current position through the relevant planning authority, title documents and a qualified planning professional before relying on it.

4. Uploaded evidence

If you provide title material, Section 32 documents, photographs or notes, they may be used to review property facts and improve the report. Uploading a document does not mean every statement in it has been independently verified. See how uploaded evidence is handled.

5. Data freshness and corrections

Property and market datasets update on different schedules. Recently completed sales, renovations, subdivisions or planning changes may not yet appear. If something is wrong, send the address, the field to correct and supporting evidence to info@aushomevalue.com.au.

6. Important limitations

  • General information only; not personal financial, legal, tax or credit advice.
  • Not a valuation prepared by a certified practising valuer and not suitable for lending, court or statutory purposes.
  • Past market movements and ranked scores do not guarantee future performance.
  • Users remain responsible for independent due diligence and decisions.
