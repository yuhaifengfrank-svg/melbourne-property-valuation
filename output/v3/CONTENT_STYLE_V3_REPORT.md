# Content Style V3 Report — Growth + Opportunity Framework

**Date:** 2026-06-11  
**Previous:** V2 → V3  
**Core change:** Raw growth stats → Growth + Opportunity + Risk balanced narrative

---

## Why V3

V2 was a huge improvement from V1 (hook-first, conversational). But raw growth data without context is misleading.

**V2 problem:**
> "Werribee 一年涨了 30%！" → Implies "high past growth = good future investment"

**V3 fix:**
> "Werribee 一年涨了 30%，墨尔本同期约 15%。涨得确实猛。但过去涨不代表未来涨——我们要看你格有没有透支、人口还在不在流入、空置率是否健康。"

---

## The 4 Mandatory Elements

Every single content piece (Xiaohongshu, video, WeChat) must include ALL 4:

### 1. Benchmark Growth

**Never state raw growth without a comparison.**

> ❌ "Werribee 过去一年涨了 25%"

> ✅ "Werribee 过去一年涨了 25%，同期墨尔本大盘约 15%。跑赢大盘 10 个百分点。"

**Benchmark sources (pick the most relevant):**
- Melbourne median growth (use ~10-15% for general, or actual suburb median)
- Greater Melbourne average
- Comparable suburb
- Top 20 average

**Canonical benchmark pattern:**
```
{suburb} 涨了 {X}%，同期墨尔本/维州 {benchmark}。{outperform/underperform} {X - benchmark} 个百分点。
```

### 2. Future Outlook — Why It May Continue

One clear bullish factor. Must anchor to real structural drivers:

| Driver | Example sentence |
|--------|-----------------|
| Population growth | "人口还在往这个方向外流，需求有支撑" |
| Infrastructure | "西区基建（火车站/医院等）还在持续落地" |
| Price gap | "跟东边同等级别的区比，价格只有 60%" |
| Supply constraint | "这个区新开发土地有限，供应不会暴增" |
| Rental demand | "空置率不到 3%，租金回报稳定" |

### 3. Risk Section — Why It May NOT Continue

Every suburb gets one primary risk factor. Be honest.

| Risk | Example sentence |
|------|-----------------|
| Price already run up | "已经涨了 25%，短期涨速可能放缓" |
| School zone weak | "学区评分只有 45，有孩子的家庭可能不考虑" |
| High vacancy | "空置率 7.6%，比墨尔本平均高，说明需求偏弱" |
| Low yield | "毛租金回报率才 2.3%，租抵供可能算不过来" |
| Commodity risk | "涨靠大盘带，不是靠自身基本面" |

**Risk section is not a disclaimer.** It's an analytical point. Show the reader you've thought about this. It builds credibility.

### 4. Never Imply Past Growth = Future Growth

**Forbidden constructions:**
- "过去一年涨了 X%，值得入手" ❌
- "这个区涨得最多，是最好选择" ❌
- "连续 3 年上涨，接下来还会涨" ❌

**Allowed, with caveat:**
- "过去涨了 X%——当然过去不代表未来，接下来要看的是..." ✅
- "数据上它持续上涨，但还需要看有没有透支" ✅
- "涨得不错，但买不买要看你能不能承受它的风险" ✅

## "Growth + Opportunity" Framework

Replace "Top Growth" with "Growth + Opportunity" across all content.

### Renaming

| Old | New |
|-----|-----|
| Top Growth Suburbs | Growth + Opportunity Suburbs |
| "涨得最快的区" | "涨得又快又稳的区" |
| Growth Score = high | Growth is ONE input, not the conclusion |
| "第 1 名涨幅" | "涨幅第一，但还要看..." |

### How Growth + Opportunity Works

```
                ┌──────────────────┐
                │   Past Growth    │  ← Historical data (benchmarked)
                │   (25% vs 15%)   │
                └────────┬─────────┘
                         │
                         ▼
                ┌──────────────────┐
                │   Future Driver  │  ← Why growth may continue
                │   (Infra / Pop)  │
                └────────┬─────────┘
                         │
                         ▼
                ┌──────────────────┐
                │   Risk Factor    │  ← Why growth may not continue
                │   (School / Run) │
                └────────┬─────────┘
                         │
                         ▼
                ┌──────────────────┐
                │  Balanced Verdict │  ← Your call, not our advice
                │  "Now you decide" │
                └──────────────────┘
```

## How This Changes Each Format

### Xiaohongshu Changes

**V2 (we use now):**
```
Deer Park 一年涨了 30% 🚀
💰 中位价 $730,000
📈 一年涨 30%，三年涨 25%
🏠 空置率 2.44%
```

**V3:**
```
Deer Park 一年涨了 30% 🚀
——墨尔本同期涨了大约 15%，跑赢一倍。

💰 中位价 $730,000，在涨幅前 10 里算中等价位
📈 一年涨 30%，三年涨 25%

为什么还能涨？
往西的人口还在走，基建持续落地。

一个要注意的数据：
学区评分 40.2。涨得快不代表什么都好。
如果是冲学区来的，这个区不太适合。

过去涨了 ≠ 未来一定涨。
但如果人口、基建、空置率都支持，那至少不是虚涨。

数据放在这了，你来判断。
```

The difference:
- ✅ Benchmark inserted after first stat
- ✅ "Why still grow" section added
- ✅ Risk section added (school score)
- ✅ "Past ≠ future" statement added
- ❌ Removed "🚀" as single indicator
- ✅ Verdict: "你来判断", not "值得入手"

### Short Video Changes

**V2 30s structure:**
```
0-3s   Hook: "涨了 30%！"
3-8s   Reveal: price + growth
8-15s  Context: why growth
15-20s Question
20-25s Risk
25-30s CTA
```

**V3 30s structure:**
```
0-3s   Hook: "涨了 30%——但这不是重点"
3-8s   Reveal: price + benchmark ("跑赢大盘 10%")
8-15s  Context: driver (why may continue)
15-20s Risk: what could stop it
20-25s Balanced takeaway: past ≠ future
25-30s CTA
```

**V3 hook change:**
> ❌ "墨尔本过去一年涨得最快的地方——你可能没听说过"
> ✅ "墨尔本过去一年涨得最快的地方——但涨得快不代表一定适合你"

### WeChat Changes

**V2 headline:**
> "3 个数字告诉你，为什么 Werribee 在悄悄涨"

**V3 headline:**
> "Werribee 涨了 25%，跑赢大盘。但你不一定该买"

Key: The headline signals balanced content, not pure promotion.

## Summary: One Rule That Changes Everything

**V2 rule:** "Never lead with Opportunity Score, lead with growth/price/school data."

**V3 rule (additional):** "Every growth statement must come with a benchmark, a future driver, a risk, and a clear 'past ≠ future' caveat."

```
V2: 数据 → Hook → Growth → CTA
V3: 数据 → Benchmark → Driver → Risk → Caveat → "You Decide"
```

The goal is not less engaging content. It's **more credible** content. Viewers who trust you because you gave them both sides will watch more, share more, and convert better.
